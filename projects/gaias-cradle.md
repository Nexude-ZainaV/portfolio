# Gaia's Cradle

A creature breeding and genetics survival simulation. You manage animal
traits, breed for survival, and adapt creatures to their environment.

My role: designer and developer.

## Systems

- Genetics and breeding with Mendelian and polygenic inheritance, mutation
  and natural selection.
- An ecosystem simulation with food webs, population dynamics, and predators
  and prey.
- Habitat restoration: build habitats, plant flora and bring in fauna until
  the environment balances.
- A realistic mode that uses real species, backed by real scientific data.
- Graphics that scale from stylised, mobile-friendly rendering up to high-end
  realistic, with free, orbit and isometric cameras.

## Stack

Unity (C#), custom shaders (ShaderLab and HLSL), Blender for 3D assets, and
Python tooling for the asset pipeline.
