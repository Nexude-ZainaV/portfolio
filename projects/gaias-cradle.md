# Gaia's Cradle

**A creature-breeding and genetics survival simulation.** Players manage
animal traits, breed for survival, and adapt creatures to their environment.

**My role:** designer and developer.

## Systems

- **Genetics and breeding** — Mendelian and polygenic inheritance, mutation
  and natural selection.
- **Ecosystem simulation** — food webs, population dynamics, predator–prey
  relationships.
- **Habitat restoration** — build habitats, plant flora and introduce fauna to
  reach a balanced environment.
- **Realistic mode** — real species backed by real scientific data.
- **Scalable graphics** — from stylised, mobile-friendly rendering up to
  high-end realistic, with free, orbit and isometric cameras.

## Stack

Unity (C#), custom shaders (ShaderLab / HLSL), Blender for 3D assets, and
Python tooling for the asset pipeline.
